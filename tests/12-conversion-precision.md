# Conversion precision

## Rational factors

Kilometres per hour = `=18 km/h to m/s | value` <!-- expect:value 5 -->
Metres per second = `=5 m/s to km/h | value` <!-- expect:value 18 -->
Minute denominator = `=1 m/min to m/s | value` <!-- expect:value 0.0167 -->
Binary large value = `=1 eib to byte | value` <!-- expect:value 1152921504606846976 -->

## Affine temperatures

Freezing Fahrenheit = `=32 F to C | value` <!-- expect:value 0 -->
Boiling Fahrenheit = `=212 F to C | value` <!-- expect:value 100 -->
Freezing Celsius = `=0 C to F | value` <!-- expect:value 32 -->

## Small converted values

Nanometre = `=1 nm to m | value` <!-- expect:value 0.000000001 -->
Angstrom = `=1 ang to m | value` <!-- expect:value 0.0000000001 -->
Light-year = `=1 ang to ly | value` <!-- expect:value 1.057 × 10⁻²⁶ -->
Electronvolt = `=1 ev to j | value` <!-- expect:value 1.6022 × 10⁻¹⁹ -->
